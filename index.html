Why voice

Because typing it out never happens.

Nobody keeps a journal about their friends. But everyone can talk for thirty seconds on the way home.

That's the whole trick: make the remembering part so easy you actually do it, and let AI handle the writing, sorting and reminding.

Before we built it

We ran it by hand for two weeks first.

Our team was the app. People sent us voice notes on WhatsApp, we transcribed every one, wrote up notes for each person they mentioned, and messaged them a briefing each morning before they met someone.

We wanted to know it actually helped before writing a single line of code.

Questions

Before you sign up

Does the person I record ever find out?

No. Nothing is ever sent to them. Your notes are yours alone — they don't know this exists.

Where do my recordings go?

Recordings stay on your phone. The audio is sent once to be turned into text and isn't kept afterwards. Only the text lives in your account, so you don't lose everything with a new phone.

Isn't this just a notes app?

A note you never re-open is the same as no note. The difference is the last step — it comes back to you thirty minutes before you walk into the room.

Do I have to talk to my phone in public?

No. There's a keyboard next to the microphone, and most people do it at home in the evening anyway.

What if the AI writes something wrong?

You see every line before it's saved, and you can edit or delete anything later. Names get misheard sometimes — fix a name once and it's fixed everywhere.

When can I use it?

Android first, in a small beta. Leave your email and we'll write when it's your turn.
